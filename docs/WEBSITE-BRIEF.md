# Vi Bi Portfolio Website Brief

## Goal

Build a responsive one-page portfolio for Van Bui, also known as Vi Bi, positioned as a **Brand & Social Media Strategist**. The site should support applications for senior brand, social, and content roles in tech, SaaS, AI, travel, and global companies.

## Core positioning

**Hero:** I turn complex products into clear, recognisable brands and social content people actually want to follow.

**Supporting line:** Brand strategy, social media, founder storytelling, creative direction, and AI-powered content systems for tech and global brands.

Social media and content must remain clearly visible. Systems thinking, technical understanding, independence, and AI use are the differentiators, not a replacement for social expertise.

## Page structure

1. Sticky navigation
2. Hero
3. Proof strip
4. What I own
5. Selected work
6. How I work
7. How I use AI
8. About
9. Contact

Use one page with concise project cards and accessible case-study dialogs. Keep case studies in this order: Gavel, Hostfully, Samson Waters.

## Visual direction

Use the supplied reference portfolio only as inspiration, not as a layout template.

- Warm cream background: `#F8EFDD`
- Near-black text and dark sections: `#0F1010`
- Mustard yellow: `#E8A81B`
- Burnt orange: `#DC451D`
- Soft white: `#FFFDF8`
- Gavel project accent: `#7557FF`
- Hostfully project accent: restrained blue
- Samson Waters project accent: restrained deep-ocean blue

Typography:

- Anton or a similar heavy condensed sans-serif for oversized statements, names, and metrics
- DM Serif Display or a similar editorial serif for section headings and quotes
- Inter or a similar clean sans-serif for body copy, navigation, and buttons

Style should feel editorial, expressive, bold, and slightly playful. Use generous whitespace, strong black section breaks, selective yellow/orange accents, pill buttons, and occasional hand-drawn-style marks. Avoid gradients, glass effects, excessive animation, overly rounded cards, and copying the reference layout.

## AI section

Explain the workflow rather than listing tools. AI helps with source extraction, research, angle exploration, repurposing, script work, visual direction, and repetitive production. Human judgment owns audience context, brand voice, fact-checking, editing, design decisions, and publication quality.
